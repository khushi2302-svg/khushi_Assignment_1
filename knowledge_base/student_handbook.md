# Fee Payment and Due Dates
Students can view their fee balance and due date through the student portal. The sample fee records in this project are demonstration data only. A student should contact the Accounts Office for confirmation of any official balance or payment receipt. This handbook does not automatically grant an extension when a payment is delayed.

# Failed Payment Portal
If the online payment portal fails, students should save a screenshot or transaction reference if available and contact the Accounts Office or IT Helpdesk. Do not retry a transaction repeatedly if the bank shows a debit but the portal shows failure. The Accounts Office must verify the transaction. This handbook does not specify an automatic next-working-day extension or waive late fees; the student should request written confirmation from the Accounts Office.

# Scholarship Applications
Scholarship eligibility and award amounts depend on the specific scheme and institutional review. Students should submit the required application and supporting documents before the published deadline. The scholarship office reviews complete applications and communicates the decision through the student portal or official student email. The agent must use the student's scholarship record for personal status and must not infer eligibility from programme alone.

# Course and Elective Changes
Students who want to change a course or elective should submit a written request to the Programme Office during the add/drop period. Approval depends on seat availability, timetable compatibility and academic rules. A change is not final until the Programme Office confirms it in writing. This sample handbook does not provide exact dates for the add/drop period.

# Support Requests and IT Helpdesk
For student portal, login, classroom technology or learning platform issues, students may contact the IT Helpdesk or submit a support request. A useful request includes the affected service, a short description of the issue and the time it occurred. Do not include passwords, one-time passwords or banking credentials in a support request.

# Academic Advising
Students seeking programme planning, academic progression or elective guidance should contact their Programme Office or assigned academic advisor. The advisor can explain course sequencing and applicable academic requirements. The sample handbook does not determine individual academic eligibility.

# Privacy and Student Information
Student records are confidential. Students should access only their own information. Passwords, one-time passwords, bank details, identity document numbers, health information and other sensitive personal details must not be stored as long-term assistant memory. Long-term memory in this demonstration is restricted to non-sensitive academic programme and learning preferences.
